# Drop anything here, with a blank line between things. A couple of minutes after you
# commit, each one is a card on the site and this file is empty again.
#
#   A link to a post on X                          goes on Words, with the post's words
#   A link to an Instagram post                    goes on And more
#   A link to an article you wrote                 goes on Words, with a copy to read on the site
#   A link to anything else you want to share      goes on And more as a Shared card
#   Just words, no link                            a thought, on Words
#   A Spotify playlist link                        becomes what Radio plays
#
# Words on the line before or after a link go on the card with it. Pictures go in the
# drop folder instead, and land on And more. Lines starting with # are ignored.
