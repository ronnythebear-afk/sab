# commit, each one is a card on the site and this file is empty again.
#
#   A link to a post on X or Instagram             goes on the Posts deck
#   A link to an article you wrote                 goes on Words, with a copy to read on the site
#   A link to anything else you want to share      goes on Words as a Shared card
#   A Spotify playlist link                        goes on Music
#   Just words, no link                            a thought, on the Posts deck
#
# Words on the line before or after a link go on the card with it. Pictures go in the
# drop folder instead. Lines starting with # are ignored.
